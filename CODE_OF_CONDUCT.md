# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and the project owner pledge to make participation
in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics,
gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic
status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing to those affected by our mistakes,
  and learning from the experience
- Focusing on what is best not just for us as individuals, but for the
  overall community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or
  advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all project community spaces, including
issues, pull requests, and discussions. It also applies when an individual is
officially representing the project in public spaces, such as through an
official project account or as an appointed representative at an event.

These standards apply to everyone participating in the project, including the
project owner.

## Enforcement

The project owner, [@bahamas10](https://github.com/bahamas10), is responsible
for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct and makes final moderation
decisions. The project owner also retains final authority over contributions
and project direction.

The project owner may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues,
and other contributions that violate this Code of Conduct, and will communicate
reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

Depending on the circumstances, responses may include a warning, removal of
content, restrictions on participation, or a temporary or permanent ban. These
actions do not need to occur in a particular order.

To report abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior, contact the
project owner privately using the contact information on
[their GitHub profile](https://github.com/bahamas10). Please avoid posting
sensitive reports in public issues or pull requests.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith, with reasonable efforts to protect the
privacy of those involved. This project is maintained by a sole owner; there
is no separate enforcement committee or independent appeals process.
